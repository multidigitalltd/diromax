# Approved exceptions

Section 1.5 of `docs/DEV-STANDARD.md`. Each exception has an owner and an expiry date; an expired exception is either fixed or approved again.

| # | Section | Exception | Reason | Risk | Compensating controls | Owner | Approved | Expires |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5.1.3 | Amounts, descriptions and notes in the client-portal ledger are not encrypted at field level. Uploaded documents are. | Totals, filters and sorting run in SQL; field encryption would move every calculation into PHP and make key loss fatal for the books. | A database dump exposes project amounts linked to clients. | Object-level authorization on every read; access and change audit log; documents (agreements, invoices — where ID numbers live) encrypted with libsodium; host disk and backup encryption required. | Multi Digital owner | 2026-11-18 | 2027-11-18 |
| 2 | 4.3.10 | Files that clients download are not scanned by an antivirus engine. | Only authenticated staff can upload; the hosting has no ClamAV daemon today. | A staff member uploads an infected file received from a supplier. | Extension and MIME allowlist plus magic-byte check; no executable or SVG types; images re-encoded; files served only as `attachment` with `nosniff`; a `dx_portal_scan_upload` filter is in place for a scanner. | Multi Digital owner | 2026-11-18 | 2027-05-18 |
| 3 | 16.6.5 | Uninstalling the portal plugin does not delete its tables and documents unless `DX_PORTAL_PURGE_ON_UNINSTALL` is defined as `true`. | They are business and financial records with a retention duty; deleting a plugin must not destroy them by accident. | Data left behind after the plugin is removed. | Scheduled jobs and transients are always removed; purge is one constant away and documented in the plugin README. | Multi Digital owner | 2026-11-18 | 2027-11-18 |
| 4 | 10.1.3 | Phase 1 of the client portal ships as one pull request larger than 400 lines. | The session works on a single branch; the PR is split into reviewable commits by layer (domain, data, security, services, admin, portal, tests). | Harder review. | Review commit by commit; 100% coverage on money and authorization code; CI gates on every commit. | Multi Digital owner | 2026-11-18 | 2026-12-31 |
